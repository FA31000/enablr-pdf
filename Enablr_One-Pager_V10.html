Business accounts & spend management

Hotel-tech SaaS
Revenue & distribution tools for hotels
Client
Challenge
Website conversion below 1% despite 10,000 monthly visits, failing to turn hotel visitors into qualified sales leads.
What we built
An interactive self-diagnosis tool letting hotels benchmark pricing, content distribution and online setup against competitors.
Result

HORECA distribution
Premium tableware & water systems, Singapore
Client
Challenge
Chasing unpaid invoices across multi-outlet hotel and F&B groups ran about two days a week, and every quotation was rebuilt by hand from supplier catalogues.
What we built
Four workstreams, each owned by an internal champion. The two priority ones: a weekly payment-chasing pass (Xero export, N1 to N5 buckets, drafts per channel, log) and a quotation assistant applying their in-house pricing formula.
Result
Chasing pass designed for under an hour instead of two days. Drafts only, and the client now runs both workflows itself.
Data centre infrastructure
Load bank testing for critical power, APAC
Starting
Challenge
Quoting a data-centre load-bank project meant assembling five pricing blocks from scattered files, then rebuilding a 25-slide proposal by hand.
What we built
An AI quoting agent co-built on live bids: a dozen input fields, a calibration engine that proposes the configuration, and the priced proposal generated.
Result
Faster, more consistent quotes without tying up an expert on routine bids. The expert validates before generation.
